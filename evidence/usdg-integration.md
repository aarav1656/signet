## 5. Regulatory framing (extends lane-academic.md section 4; not legal advice)

GENIUS s.4 bars issuer yield "solely in connection with the holding, use, or retention" of the coin; MiCA Art. 50 bars issuer or third-party rewards "with an effect equivalent to that of interest." A per-transaction fee or premium paid by a counterparty for risk taken is neither: it is priced from real activity, not from holding the token, and is not owed regardless of outcome. That is activity-based compensation for risk, the lane the OCC NPRM and Senate draft leave open; Paxos is not a party. README: never "earn on USDG", "USDG yield" or any balance-based rate. State Paxos and GDN are unaffiliated; any GDN rewards stay in treasury.
